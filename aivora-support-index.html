❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Tap "New Account" on the login screen. Enter a username (4-15 characters) and password (6+ characters).

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Please contact us via email or our Zalo community group with your username. We'll help you reset your password manually for security reasons.

The game won't connect to the server.

Please check:

  • Your internet connection is stable
  • You're using the latest version of the app
  • Try restarting the app

If issues persist, contact us with your device model, OS version, and a description of the problem.

How do I report a bug?

Send us an email with:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15 Pro)
  • iOS version (e.g., iOS 17.4)
  • App version (visible in the login screen)
  • Description of the bug and steps to reproduce
  • Screenshots if possible

Can I play across multiple devices?

Yes! Your account progress is synced across all devices when you log in with the same credentials.

How do I delete my account?

Send us an email from the email address associated with your account. We'll process the deletion within 7 days.
